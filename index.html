CLARIFICATIONS

Frequently answered questions

Clear information regarding paper stock, edition numbers, worldwide shipping, and licensing.

What paper do you print on?

We print exclusively on Hahnemuhle Photo Rag 310 gsm, a 100 percent cotton archival paper with a smooth matte texture that eliminates glare.

Are these prints limited edition?

Yes. Landscape masterworks are limited to editions of 25. Each print is hand numbered and accompanied by a signed certificate of authenticity.

How are prints packaged for transit?

Unframed prints are rolled in archival tissue and shipped inside heavy industrial tubes. Framed works ship flat inside custom wooden crates with reinforced corners.

Can I license these images for commercial campaigns?

Yes. Commercial licenses for digital campaigns, print media, and billboards can be requested directly through our licensing form.

What happens if my package arrives damaged?

We replace any damaged delivery at zero cost. Simply email a photograph of the damaged crate within 48 hours of delivery.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We ship worldwide with tracked DHL Express courier delivery.

Can I request a custom size for a specific wall?

Yes. We regularly produce custom proportions up to 60 inches on the long edge for interior architecture projects.

Can I view RAW files before licensing?

Commercial buyers can review full resolution watermarked previews before purchasing commercial usage rights.
